# Stanford and MIT Reinforcement Learning Courses

## Stanford University – CS234: Reinforcement Learning (Winter 2025)

- **Platform:** Stanford Online
- **Format:** Lecture-based with assignments and a course project
- **Content:** Covers core RL concepts, including exploration, generalization, and deep RL techniques
- **Prerequisites:** Proficiency in Python, linear algebra, and basic probability
- **Ideal For:** Students seeking a comprehensive introduction to RL
- **Links:**

  - [Course Page](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs234/)
  - [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/tallamjr/stanford-cs234)
  - [Stanford Explore Courses](https://explorecourses.stanford.edu/search?academicYear=20182019&filter-coursestatus-Active=on&q=CS+234%3A+Reinforcement+Learning&view=catalog)

## MIT – 6.S191: Introduction to Deep Learning (2024 Edition)

- **Platform:** YouTube
- **Format:** Lecture-based
- **Content:** Introduces deep learning methods with applications in computer vision, robotics, and more
- **Ideal For:** Learners interested in deep learning foundations
- **Links:**

  - [Lecture 5: Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8JVRbHAVCws)
  - [Intro to Deep Learning](https://introtodeeplearning.com/)
  - [Professional MIT Education](https://professional.mit.edu/course-catalog/reinforcement-learning)

## MIT – 6.S7950: Advanced Reinforcement Learning

- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Format:** Lecture-based
- **Content:** Explores advanced RL topics, including deep RL and applications
- **Prerequisites:** Graduate or advanced undergraduate level
- **Ideal For:** Learners with a solid foundation in RL seeking to delve deeper
- **Links:**

  - [Course Page](https://web.mit.edu/6.7950/www/)

## MIT – Professional Education: Reinforcement Learning

- **Platform:** MIT Professional Education
- **Format:** 3-day intensive course
- **Content:** Covers theoretical frameworks and practical tools for implementing RL in real-world scenarios
- **Ideal For:** Professionals looking to apply RL in organizational contexts
- **Links:**

  - [Course Page](https://professional.mit.edu/course-catalog/reinforcement-learning)
